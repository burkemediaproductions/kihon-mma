Why Adults Train

More than a workout.

Kihon gives adults a place to train hard, learn practical skills and leave the rest of the day at the door.

01

Fitness with a Purpose

Conditioning happens while you are learning striking, grappling, movement and control.

02

Mental Reset

Rounds, drills and technical focus demand your attention and can give you a real break from the noise of the day.

03

Real Skill

Progress is measurable. You learn techniques, apply them, make mistakes, adjust and get better.

04

Community

You train with people who know what it feels like to be new and who want to see you improve.

05

Confidence

Capability changes the way you carry yourself. Training builds confidence from experience, not hype.

06

Competition — If You Want It

Adults who want to test themselves can train toward tournaments and competition, while recreational students remain equally welcome.

Start Where You Are

Beginners belong here.

Many adults hesitate because they think they are too old, too out of shape or too inexperienced. Those are reasons to begin, not reasons to wait.

Kihon coaches break techniques down, training partners help one another and progress happens one class at a time. You are not expected to know what you are doing on day one.

Adult Training Options

Choose a discipline. Build a complete game.

Adult classes throughout the week let students focus on a favorite discipline or cross-train across several.

01

Muay Thai

Striking, pad work, combinations, defense and conditioning.

02

Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu / Judo

Gi grappling, positional control, submissions, throws and transitions.

03

No-Gi / Submission Wrestling

Fast-paced grappling without the gi, focused on control, movement and submissions.

04

MMA

Integrated striking and grappling for students who want a broader combat-sports skill set.
