Competition at Kihon

Earn the opportunity. Prepare for the moment.

Competition is not required to train at Kihon. For students who choose it, however, preparation becomes more demanding. Conditioning, technical details, strategy, consistency and composure all matter.

Kihon competitors have traveled nationally and internationally for martial arts events, giving serious students the chance to pursue goals beyond the local training room.

The Competition Mindset

More than being tough.

A strong competitor learns to prepare before pressure arrives.

01

Consistency

You cannot cram for a fight or tournament. Progress comes from showing up and stacking good sessions together.

02

Technical Depth

Competition exposes gaps quickly. Students learn to refine details rather than depend on a favorite move.

03

Conditioning

Skill needs a body that can keep working when the pace rises.

04

Composure

The ability to think, listen and adjust under pressure is part of the skill set.

05

Teamwork

Competitors represent more than themselves. Teammates and coaches are part of the preparation and the result.

06

Perspective

Winning matters — but the larger goal is becoming a more capable martial artist through the process.

For the Next Generation

A pathway young competitors can grow into.

Kihon has a history of developing young martial artists who compete in grappling and combat-sport events. That path begins with the same fundamentals taught to every student: discipline, respect, technique and consistency.

Parents and coaches work together to decide when competition makes sense for an individual student.
