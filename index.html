More Than a Martial Arts School

Everything starts with the fundamentals.

At Kihon MMA & Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, martial arts is the foundation — but personal growth is the goal.

Kihon is a well-rounded martial arts school serving Desert Hot Springs, the Palm Springs area and the Coachella Valley through Karate, Muay Thai kickboxing, Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, Judo, submission wrestling and MMA training.

Students learn practical skills while developing confidence, discipline, physical fitness, respect and the ability to keep going when something is difficult.

Whether you're bringing your child to a first class, looking for a better way to stay fit, learning self-defense or preparing to compete, you don't have to arrive experienced. You just have to start.

Before Your First Class

Questions are part of the process.

Here are a few of the things new students and parents ask most often.

Do I need martial arts experience before joining Kihon?

No. Kihon welcomes complete beginners as well as experienced martial artists and competitors. Coaches help students learn fundamentals and progress at an appropriate pace.

What martial arts does Kihon teach?

Kihon offers training that includes Muay Thai kickboxing, Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, Judo, submission wrestling, Karate and MMA.

Does Kihon offer classes for kids and adults?

Yes. Kihon offers youth programs beginning at age four as well as adult martial arts classes. Current class times and age groups are listed on the Classes & Schedule page.

Where is Kihon MMA located?

Kihon MMA & Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu is located at 12580 Palm Drive in Desert Hot Springs, California 92240.
