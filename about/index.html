The Kihon Standard

Martial arts is the foundation. Growth is the goal.

Kihon MMA & Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu is a well-rounded martial arts school serving Desert Hot Springs, Palm Springs and the Coachella Valley. Students train across Muay Thai, Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, Judo, Karate, submission wrestling and MMA.

The environment is structured without being intimidating. Students are expected to work, listen, improve and respect the people around them — while coaches meet them where they are and help them progress.

Sensei Eddie Bates

Coach. Competitor. Mentor. Community Leader.

Sensei Eddie brings decades of martial arts study, coaching and competition experience into a teaching style that challenges students while keeping growth personal.

01

Technical Foundation

Students learn why a technique works, not simply how to copy it. Fundamentals come first so skills hold up under pressure.

02

Personal Accountability

Training asks students to show up, stay consistent and take responsibility for their progress inside and outside the dojo.

03

Competition Experience

Kihon students who want to compete can train toward local, national and international opportunities with experienced coaching behind them.

04

Youth Development

For younger students, confidence, discipline, focus, respect and resilience matter just as much as physical technique.

05

Family Culture

Parents train with children, teammates become friends and students are encouraged to help newer people find their footing.

06

A Place to Grow

You do not have to arrive fit, experienced or fearless. Kihon is a place to become more capable over time.

Desert Hot Springs

Built here. Growing here.

Kihon is deeply connected to the Desert Hot Springs community. The school has spent years working with local children, adults and families while helping students carry the lessons of martial arts into school, work and home life.

The team has also traveled beyond the Coachella Valley to compete and represent Kihon at events across the country and abroad.
