Privacy at Kihon.

This policy explains how information submitted through the Kihon MMA website may be collected and used.

基本

Last updated: August 11, 2026

Information You Provide

When you submit a contact or trial-class form, Kihon may receive information such as your name, email address, phone number, training interest and the message you choose to send.

How Information Is Used

Information submitted through this website may be used to respond to questions, help you choose a class, communicate about training, schedule a visit or provide information you requested.

Website Hosting & Form Processing

This website may use third-party hosting and form-processing services to deliver the site and securely transmit form submissions. Those providers may process technical information necessary to operate their services.

Analytics & Technical Data

If analytics are enabled, the website may collect general technical information such as browser type, device type, referring page and how visitors use the site. Kihon does not use this website to intentionally collect sensitive personal information.

External Links

The site may link to services such as Google Maps, Facebook and Instagram. Those services operate under their own privacy policies.

Your Choices

You may contact Kihon at kihonmmaca@gmail.com to ask about information you have submitted through the website.
