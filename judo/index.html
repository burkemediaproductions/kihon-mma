Train the Discipline

Judo at Kihon.

Kihon teaches Judo as part of a well-rounded martial arts program. Students learn the fundamentals carefully, practice with partners and build toward more realistic application as their skill grows.

You can train this discipline as your primary focus or use it to complement other Kihon classes. Beginners are welcome, and experienced students can continue refining their game in a room that values technical progress over ego.
