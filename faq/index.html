Kihon FAQ

Good questions are part of starting.

If your question is not here, call or send a message. The team will help you figure it out.

Do I need martial arts experience?

No. Beginners are welcome. Coaches will help you understand the class structure and start with the fundamentals.

Do I need to get in shape before I start?

No. Fitness develops through training. Start where you are and build from there.

What does Kihon offer for kids?

Youth options include Mini Ninjas MMA, Little Ninjas MMA, Intermediate Ninjas MMA, Kids Muay Thai and Kids Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu / Judo.

What do adults train?

Adult classes include Muay Thai, Brazilian Gi Jiu-Jitsu / Judo, No-Gi Jiu-Jitsu & Submission Wrestling, and Muay Thai / MMA.

Do I have to compete?

No. Competition is available for students who want that path, but many people train for fitness, confidence, skill, stress relief and community.

What should I wear to my first class?

Wear clean athletic clothing that allows you to move comfortably. Contact Kihon before your visit if you want class-specific guidance.

Can parents train too?

Yes. Kihon has families who train together, and parents are welcome to explore the adult program while their children build their own skills.

Where is Kihon located?

Kihon MMA & Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu is located at 12580 Palm Drive in Desert Hot Springs, California 92240.

How do I try a class?

Use the contact form, call (760) 272-0019 or email kihonmmaca@gmail.com. Tell us who will be training and what you are interested in.
