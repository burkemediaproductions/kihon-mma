Why Parents Choose Kihon

Skills for martial arts. Habits for life.

Every class is physical, but the benefits reach much farther than fitness.

01

Confidence

Students learn to handle challenges, solve problems and become more comfortable with what they can do.

02

Discipline

Following directions, staying in line, repeating technique and working through difficulty become part of the routine.

03

Respect

Students practice respect for coaches, teammates, themselves and the responsibility that comes with learning martial arts.

04

Coordination

Striking, grappling, movement and balance help young students become more aware and capable in their bodies.

05

Self-Defense

Age-appropriate training teaches practical awareness and skills while reinforcing that martial arts should be used responsibly.

06

Belonging

Children train with teammates, build friendships and learn that improvement happens faster when people help one another.

Ages & Pathways

A place to start — and room to grow.

Kihon offers age-appropriate youth classes during the week, including Mini Ninjas MMA for ages 4–5, Little Ninjas MMA for ages 6–8, Intermediate Ninjas MMA for ages 9–12, Kids Muay Thai and Kids Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu / Judo.

Some students come for confidence and an active routine. Others discover that they love competition. The program supports both paths without requiring a child to become a competitor.

What Training Looks Like

Structured. Active. Encouraging.

A strong youth class balances fundamentals, movement, repetition and fun.

01

Listen & Learn

Students begin with instruction and clear expectations before techniques are practiced.

02

Practice with Purpose

Drills build coordination and skill through repetition rather than rushing ahead.

03

Apply the Skill

Controlled partner work gives students a chance to use what they learned safely and realistically.

04

Grow Together

Coaches and teammates reinforce effort, accountability and helping the people around you improve.
