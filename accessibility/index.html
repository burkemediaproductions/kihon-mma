Everyone Should Be Able to Use This Site.

Kihon MMA is committed to providing a website experience that is usable by as many people as possible.

基本

Last updated: August 11, 2026

Our Approach

The Kihon website is designed with semantic HTML, keyboard-accessible navigation, visible focus states, descriptive alternative text where appropriate, responsive layouts and support for reduced-motion preferences.

Ongoing Improvement

Accessibility is an ongoing process. Content, browsers, devices and assistive technologies change, so the site may be reviewed and improved over time.

Need Help?

If you have difficulty accessing information or completing a task on this website, contact Kihon and we will work to provide the information another way.
