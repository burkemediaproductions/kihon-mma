His Kihon Journey

He started because of his son.

Leo originally came to Kihon looking for something positive and active for his son. After watching him train, compete and improve, Leo found himself drawn to Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu and decided to step onto the mat too.

Training became more than exercise. Leo gained confidence, found an outlet after difficult days at work and developed the knowledge to better understand and support his son's martial arts journey.

Most importantly, martial arts gave father and son something they could practice, talk about and look back on together.
